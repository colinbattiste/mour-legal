End users opt in to receive MOUR one-time passcode (OTP) SMS messages only inside the MOUR mobile app (com.mour.app) during login or signup. The user voluntarily enters their own mobile phone number on the in-app phone verification screen and taps a control such as "Send code" or "Continue" to request a verification text. At that step the app shows consent disclosures stating that by requesting a code the user agrees to receive a one-time MOUR authentication SMS; messages are transactional OTPs sent only when the user requests them (message frequency: on-demand / as needed; not recurring marketing); message and data rates may apply; reply HELP for help; reply STOP to opt out. Consent is collected specifically for this OTP campaign and is not obtained from purchased or shared lead lists; consent is not a condition of purchase. There is no website form opt-in and no keyword text-in opt-in for this campaign. Public evidence of the in-app opt-in screen (HTML mock of the phone verification UI with the required disclosures; no login required): https://colinbattiste.github.io/mour-legal/sms-otp-consent.html. We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
